Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Lowtides is a demo. It uses test money in the PayPal sandbox and fictional stores. Here is what it keeps and what it does not.

What we never collect

  • Card numbers or bank details. There is no place to type one.
  • Real payments. No real money moves.

Visiting without signing in

Your browser gets one cookie so you have a private copy of the demo. That copy lives in the server’s memory and is deleted after about two hours without use. The server also sees your network address for a short time to limit abuse.

If you make an account

Accounts are optional. If you make one, our sign-in provider (Supabase) keeps your email address and a scrambled form of your password. Lowtides itself never stores your password. Your demo dashboard (rules, store settings, receipts and test purchases) is saved to your account so it is there next time.

Talk to a human

If you send a help request, we keep your email, your message and the chat you had with the guide, so a person can answer. Your email is used only to answer that request.

Other services this site uses

  • PayPal sandbox: test payments carry demo store names and test amounts, not your details.
  • AI answers: when the guide answers with AI, the question you typed is sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Do not type private details into the guide.
  • Voice: the Talk button uses your browser’s own speech service, which may send audio to your browser’s maker. When the natural voice is switched on, Talk instead opens a short voice call with an AI assistant through Vapi, which processes what you say in order to answer. We turn call recording off, and each call ends by itself after three minutes.
  • Video: the video plays from YouTube only after you press play.
  • Fonts: some pages load fonts from Google Fonts.

No selling, no ads

We do not sell personal information and this site shows no ads.

Seeing or deleting your information

Press Talk to a human at the top of any page and ask. We will delete your account and its saved dashboard, or your help request, on request.
