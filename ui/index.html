                Get a price before the work starts
                
      
                Like a contractor's estimate. Lowtides quotes the job from what similar jobs really cost. Hold the quote on PayPal, and when the job is done you pay only the real cost. The rest is released.
                
      
                
        
        
        
        
      
                
      
      
                
    
                
    
                
      
                Jobs
                
      
                
      
                Agents add calls to a job by sending the header X-Lowtides-Task: job-id. Charges round to the nearest cent, and anything under half a cent is free.
                
    
                
  
                

  
  
                
    
                
      
                Prepaid allowance
                
      
                Turn on "Pay from wallet" for an agent and it spends only what you've loaded. When the wallet is empty, it stops. No surprise bills.
                
      
                
    
                
  
                

  
  
                
    
                
      
                Bill clients for the AI you run for them
                
      
                Give each client their own agents. Lowtides totals what their agents cost, adds your markup, and sends a PayPal invoice with the savings receipt on it.
