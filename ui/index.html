    What the cop noticed
    
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      Get a price before the work starts
      
      
      Like a contractor's estimate. CostCop quotes the job from what similar jobs really cost. Hold the quote on PayPal, and when the job is done you pay only the real cost. The rest is released.
      
      
      
        
        
        
        
      
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Jobs
      
      
      
      
      Agents add calls to a job by sending the header X-CostCop-Task: job-id. Charges round to the nearest cent, and anything under half a cent is free.
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      Prepaid allowance
      
      
      Turn on "Pay from wallet" for an agent and it spends only what you've loaded. When the wallet is empty, it stops. No surprise bills.
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      Bill clients for the AI you run for them
      
      
      Give each client their own agents. CostCop totals what their agents cost, adds your markup, and sends a PayPal invoice with the savings receipt on it.

      Models and prices
      
      
      Prices are per 1 million tokens. Check them against your provider's pricing page: CostCop's dollar math is only as right as these numbers.
