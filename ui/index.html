Meet GuardPooch

A watchdog for fake stores.

Scam stores copy a real store’s name with one letter off. GuardPooch checks the spelling, how new the web address is, whether the store signed its rules, and prices that look too good. A store that fails gets a red light, before any money moves.

Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

What is Lowtides?

Lowtides lets an AI shopper buy things for you without giving it free rein over your money. Your rules travel with the shopper as a signed Pass, each store shows its own rules, and nothing is paid unless both sides say yes.

Is this real money?

No. This is a demo. Payments run in the PayPal sandbox with test money, and the stores are fictional demo stores. Nothing here charges a real card or account.

What is a Pass?

Your Pass is a signed note your AI shopper carries. It says what it may buy, how much it may spend and when it must ask you first. Stores can check it, and you can cancel it at any time.

What happens when my rules and the store’s rules don’t match?

Nothing is paid. If the order only needs a person to say okay, it waits for your yes (yellow). If it breaks a rule on either side, it shows red: no match, and no money moves.

How does GuardPooch spot a fake store?

GuardPooch checks the store before your shopper pays: names spelled one letter off from a real store, web addresses that are brand new, store rules that are not signed, and prices that look too good to be true. A store that fails gets a red light.

How do I stop everything?

Press the big red button. Stop all revokes your Pass at every store at once, so your AI shopper cannot buy anything else until you turn it back on.
