# Demo video script (2:50)

Record the screen at 1080p with your voice over it. Browser zoom 100%. Open https://lowtides.ai and press **Reset demo** first, so the runaway agent is loaded and two purchases are waiting.
Rules: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, shows the project working, no copyrighted music (your voice or silence is safest), no third-party logos beyond PayPal's own product names.

Each beat names the judging criterion it serves, so nothing is wasted. The flow is the same one a judge gets on the live site: replay, then "Now you try", then approve, KILL.

---

**0:00–0:12. Open on the runaway agent (Impact)**
Home screen: the night-shift agent is PAUSED, "$499 protected today", the red chips (scam payment held, over-limit purchase blocked, runaway loop stopped).
"At 2 a.m. this AI agent got stuck in a loop, tried to rent a $480 GPU, and was talked into paying a stranger. It didn't cost me a cent. Here's why."

**0:12–0:25. The problem (Impact)**
Cut to /why/ stats for two seconds, then back.
"Agents spend money two ways: what they cost to run, and what they buy. Nobody should hand an agent a wallet without a budget and a STOP button."

**0:25–1:20. The replay (Technology, Innovation, Presentation)**
Press the red **▶ Watch a runaway agent get stopped** on the banner. It plays live against the real API (about 52 seconds). Narrate over it, briefly:
- "I write the rules in plain English. Lowtides turns them into strict rules. The AI never decides a payment. Plain code does."
- "A $12 data purchase inside my rules: approved and paid through PayPal Payouts."
- "A poisoned web page tells it to pay 'Refund Desk.' New payee plus injection-style text: held for me."
- "A $480 GPU rental: over the per-purchase limit, blocked. An 80,000-token data dump: blocked by the leash."
- "The same request again and again: the loop guard pauses it. Then KILL denies what's waiting, pulls back the payout PayPal hasn't delivered, and revokes its saved PayPal."

**1:20–1:30. "Now you try" (Design, Presentation)**
The replay ends on **Now you try** with three things to do. Press **▶ Now you try: approve or deny the $45 dataset**. It reloads the same night, stopped just before the KILL, and lands on Approvals.
"It doesn't end on a dead screen. It hands the controls to you."

**1:30–1:55. Approve (Design, Technology)**
Approve the **$45 dataset** with one tap. Then tap **Approve** on the **$19 Refund Desk** card: a dialog asks "Approve a possible prompt injection?" with **Deny it** as the default. Press **Deny it**.
"Normal over-limit buys are one tap. A purchase that smells like prompt injection is never one tap: Deny is the default."

**1:55–2:15. PayPal on file (Innovation, Technology)**
Open the **Wallet** tab: the agent's **PayPal on file** shows connected (a PayPal Vault payment token, simulated in the demo). Back on Home, press **Resume** so the agent is live again.
"Each agent can have a saved PayPal payment method, so it can top up without a checkout page. That's exactly the thing you need to be able to take back."

**2:15–2:35. KILL (Impact, Design)**
Press **KILL** on the agent, then **Kill it now**. Show the kill summary, then **Wallet**: PayPal on file reads **revoked**.
"One button: the agent stops, waiting purchases are denied, open PayPal holds are voided, unclaimed payouts are cancelled, and its saved PayPal payment method is revoked at PayPal."

**2:35–2:50. Close (Impact, Presentation)**
Quick cut to the PayPal sandbox payout ID matching the evidence pack (optional), then Home at low tide.
"Lowtides. One budget and a STOP button for AI agents. Open source, live right now at lowtides.ai. Keep AI spend at low tide."

**Recording tips**
- Run the replay once before recording so you know its timing (about 52 seconds, 10 steps at 5 seconds each). Use **Skip step** if you're running long.
- Keep the cursor slow and point at what you're talking about.
- Upload to YouTube as Public, title: "Lowtides: one budget and a STOP button for AI agents (PayPal AI Hackathon)".
